OUR LANDSCAPE

Picturesque
Foothills

The beautiful Ashgabat Golf Club course perfectly fits into the natural landscape of the picturesque foothills of Ashgabat. The golf club's territory covers 70 hectares.

On the way to the holes, there are murmuring waterfalls, ponds with ducks, forests, pheasants, partridges, foxes and hares — a sanctuary where sport and nature become one.

◈ Murmuring Waterfalls
◈ Wildlife Ponds
◈ Natural Forests
◈ Scenic Foothills

SKILLS & TRAINING

Academy & Training Grounds

World-class facilities designed for every level — from first-time beginners to seasoned professionals refining their craft.

01

Academic Field

The academic field provides an opportunity to gain practical experience for both beginners and experienced golfers. A full-range facility built to elevate every swing.

02

Short Game Focus

The Putting Green and Chipping Green allow you to fully focus on practicing the short game — precision, touch, and confidence under pressure.

03

The Bunker

Master the art of taking the ball out of a deep sand trap. Our bunker training zone is purpose-built for learning one of golf's most demanding skills.

OASIS CLUB
GROWING THE GAME

Popularization of Golf

Annually, the Ashgabat Golf Club organizes international, thematic, corporate and weekly regular tournaments, implements programs to promote golf among beginners, trains adults and children in golf schools, and conducts a summer children's golf camp.

Junior Golf Camp

Summer children's golf camp where the next generation discovers the sport in a nurturing, fun environment.

Tournaments & Events

International, thematic, corporate and weekly regular competitions that bring the global golf community together.

Golf School

Structured programs teaching adults and children the fundamentals and finer points of the game year-round.

WORLDWIDE COMMUNITY

International Recognition

The beautiful and interesting course of the club was loved by both Turkmen and golfers from around the world. Golf enthusiasts from the USA, England, Poland, Russia, India, Malaysia, Japan, Korea, Pakistan and many more have become members — a testament to the club's recognition at a high international level.
